A broker, not a lender.

Creditplus doesn’t lend money itself. We act as a credit broker: you tell us about the car you want and what you can afford, and we introduce you to lenders on our panel who may be able to offer you finance.

Because we work with more than one lender, we can look at a range of options for you rather than a single deal.

Working with a panel of lenders.

We work with a panel of lenders who offer Hire Purchase and PCP finance. When you choose to get a quote, you’ll be taken to a short online application, where you can see what may be available.
