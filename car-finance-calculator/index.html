What moves your payment.

  • Deposit: a bigger deposit means you borrow less.
  • Term: longer terms lower the monthly cost but increase the total cost of credit.
  • Credit profile: the better your credit history, the lower the rate you may be offered.
  • PCP: deferring part of the cost to a final payment lowers the monthly payment.

About the calculator.

It’s an illustration based on the figures you enter. Your actual payment depends on the lender, the rate you are offered, and your circumstances, so treat it as a guide rather than a quote.

APR stands for Annual Percentage Rate. It’s the yearly cost of borrowing, including interest and mandatory fees, shown as a percentage. The rate you’re offered depends on your circumstances.

On a PCP, part of the cost is deferred to a final amount at the end of the agreement. Paying it lets you keep the car. A bigger final payment means lower monthly payments, but you’ll owe more at the end.
