Car finance, explained.

Spreading the cost of a car can make it more affordable. Here is how the main options work, and what to expect when you apply.

What is car finance?

Car finance lets you pay for a vehicle over time instead of all at once. You choose the car, a lender pays the dealer, and you repay the lender in monthly instalments, plus interest.

Creditplus is a credit broker, not a lender. We introduce you to lenders on our panel who may be able to offer you finance. All finance is subject to status and income.

Hire Purchase or PCP?

Both spread the cost. The right choice depends on whether you want to own the car and how much you want to pay each month.

FeatureHire Purchase (HP)Personal Contract Purchase (PCP)
Who owns the car?You, once the final payment is madeYou, only if you pay the final amount
Monthly paymentsUsually higher, as you repay the full costUsually lower, as some cost is deferred
At the endCar is yours, nothing more to payPay the final amount, return or part-exchange
Mileage limitsNoneYes, charges apply if exceeded
Best if…You want to keep the car long termYou like to change car every few years

Car finance with less-than-perfect credit.

A patchy credit history doesn’t always rule you out. Different lenders look at different things.

Our panel includes lenders who consider a range of credit situations, so it can be worth finding out what’s available. Lenders typically look at your income, your outgoings, your credit history and how much you want to borrow.

No lender can guarantee approval, and all finance is subject to status and affordability checks. If you are accepted, the rate offered may be higher than for someone with a stronger credit history.

Ways to improve your chances

  • Check your credit file for errors and get them corrected
  • Make sure you’re on the electoral roll at your current address
  • Consider a larger deposit, or a less expensive car
  • Avoid making several credit applications in a short time
  • Keep up with existing payments
