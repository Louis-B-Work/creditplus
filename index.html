Two popular ways to finance a car.

Here is how they differ.

Hire Purchase (HP)

Pay a deposit, then fixed monthly payments over an agreed term. Once the final payment is made, the car is yours.

  • You own the car at the end
  • Fixed monthly payments make budgeting simple
  • No mileage limits
Get my quote→

Personal Contract Purchase (PCP)

Lower monthly payments, with a final amount due at the end if you want to keep the car. Or hand it back or part-exchange it.

  • Usually lower monthly payments than HP
  • Choose at the end: keep, return or swap
  • Agreed annual mileage applies
